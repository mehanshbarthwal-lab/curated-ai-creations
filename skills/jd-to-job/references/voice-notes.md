# Voice notes for outreach emails

Mehansh's natural writing voice is conversational and flowing rather than clipped — long sentences
strung together with "and," "so," "which," "as," loose comma use, rarely breaking into short
punchy fragments. In professional contexts he stays wordy rather than crisp, and tends to
over-explain a little rather than under-explain, though an outreach email should still stay under
maybe 150-200 words since a founder is skimming it.

When drafting the outreach email:
- write it as one flowing note, not bullet points
- avoid corporate-sounding openers like "I am writing to express my interest in..."
- let the reasoning behind the project show through naturally in a sentence or two, rather than a
  separate "why I built this" section
- don't over-polish it into something that sounds AI-generated or templated — a few genuine,
  slightly informal turns of phrase are better than perfect crispness here, since the whole point
  of this strategy is coming across as a real person who thought about the role, not a form letter
- close warmly, inviting a conversation rather than asking to be considered

Always present the email as a draft and ask what to adjust — tone, specifics, level of detail —
rather than treating it as finished copy.
